FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as a volunteer? +

Simply fill in the volunteer registration form on our Volunteer page. It takes under 3 minutes. Our coordinator will contact you within 48 hours.

Is volunteering free of cost? +

Yes, completely free! Agrapathik covers all event logistics. At most field events, we also provide meals and refreshments for our volunteers.

Can organizations partner with Agrapathik? +

Absolutely! We welcome corporate, NGO, and government partnerships. Use the contact form above with the subject "Partnership / Sponsorship" and we'll arrange a meeting.

How can I donate to Agrapathik? +

We accept donations via bKash, bank transfer, and cheque. Email us at donate@Agrapathik.org or call us directly to arrange a donation. 100% of donations go directly to programmes.
